## Dependency verdict

Completeness: useful bounded research substrate, incomplete for forward evaluation and the full trading design. Clarity: strong distinction between arithmetic, registration and evidence in current architecture; high-level startup/owner convenience can obscure authority and keyed recovery obligations. Reliability: source and upstream tests support bounded replay/reconstruction claims, but external consumer, shared-journal contention and cross-system recovery remain unverified. Usability: installable client and project generator reduce setup burden; checkout-based host and multi-researcher composition need an independent sibling example. Effort cannot honestly be measured in coding hours or lines before implementation.

No finding licenses reading internal ledgers or copying governance. F-01–F-07 in [review](review.md) are the upstream follow-up register; domain-specific Murmur adapters and portfolio controls belong here rather than upstream by default.
