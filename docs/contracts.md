Status: proposed contracts under [M-CV1](constitution.md). IDs below define proposed transitions; numeric fixtures are not runtime grants. W-2 retains the manual-owner baseline. The current proposed autonomous alternative is **DA-1 in C-12/C-13**, replayed in [W-3](away-replay.md). Its explicit changes to routine admission, fencing and duration supersede the baseline only within that proposed mode; no proposal is admitted by this routing note.

## C-01 — mandate and scheduling

Proposed M-1: fictional liquid instruments AAA and BBB, long-only cash equities, no leverage, no external provider or actual spending. Paper starting cash $100,000; gross ≤40% NAV, each underlying ≤25%, each strategy ≤20%; daily target turnover ≤20% NAV. Keep cash ≥60%, including fees and pending buys. Research ceiling 12.00 synthetic units per cycle: one shared Research grant of 10.00 naming both distinct researcher identities, each lineage capped at 10.00, and a separate Checker grant of 2.00; no automatic replenishment or transfer. This replaces the draft fixed per-researcher partition: it must exercise genuine contention. Owner approves the finite run of two historical slots before execution. Each slot runs at virtual 18:00 UTC after its input cutoff; duplicate trigger returns the same cycle. No time-zone ambiguity or automatic catch-up of missed slots: record missed, owner may authorize one named replacement slot. Unused funds remain unused.

The 20% turnover limit applies to ordinary rebalancing. Required exits use only the separately bounded, still-proposed exception in C-05; their turnover and costs remain visible. V-01 consumer validation does not adopt M-1 or exercise this exception.

For the finite two-cycle desk rehearsal, owner approval enumerates both slots and their separate grants in advance: G-R1/G-R2 each10.00 and G-C1/G-C2 each2.00, total24.00 synthetic units. A slot can use only its named, legitimately admitted ARH grants; there is no automatic budget reset. Prior charges remain in lineage usage; the10.00 per-lineage ceiling across research grants still applies. Finish/close/fence the prior cycle before enabling the next; unused allowance is neither carried into nor added to the next grant. If an old liability or funding control is unresolved, the next cycle waits. Rehearsal opening state assumes checked setup already exists, with its historical accounting shown separately; this is not a claim that startup is free or implemented. Runtime startup must retain all setup charges under V-01.

More precisely, W-2 begins with both cycles' grants already admitted/applied in ARH; no delayed Apply is presumed valid across changing bases. The Murmur scheduler owns the finite slot gate and cannot dispatch cycle2 before its slot/prior closure, even though ARH's public budget reports that pre-admitted grant's capacity. At the slot, re-read current upstream authority and record a local dispatch eligibility event; do not invent an ARH activation command or charge a nonexistent control check. If a grant is no longer active, stop for legitimate new owner controls. Show “future-slot funds, withheld from dispatch” separately from ARH spendable balance. This is scheduling authority, not a competing budget ledger or a claim that ARH enforces the local calendar.

W-2 explicitly proposes a Checker lineage ceiling4.50 across its historical setup0.50 plus two2.00 grants; the separate pool caps still apply. Ending those research slots closes research funding, not automatically the portfolio. W-2 proposes retaining monitoring and already-authorized exits while existing positions remain, with no new ordinary rebalance or research slot after the finite run. Owner acceptance of this holding-duration choice is unresolved (U-W2-03); the alternative is an explicitly authorized end-of-session exit, never an implicit flatten.

Reservation closure and grant fencing are different: grantees close their own completed reservations through ARH; only the upstream Principal fences grants. W-2 makes end-of-cycle fencing an explicit owner action (OD-05 and OD-08), not an inferred scheduler permission. If the owner has not fenced, the local finite schedule still forbids new dispatch; the workspace must report upstream grants as unfenced rather than inventing closure.

Murmur scheduler records CY-1 with M-1, slot, actor plan, exact input manifest, operation names and expected revisions before requesting work. A single scheduler writer per run holds exclusive cycle dispatch; researchers may execute distinct operations concurrently. This is a proposed deployment restriction, not a repair of ARH journal concurrency. Startup reconciles unfinished cycles before creating a new one. A missing checker budget stops before unreviewable research is dispatched. Changing the budget requires both owner mandate amendment and legitimate ARH grant changes; their partial completion blocks new work until both agree. No distributed atomicity is assumed.

## C-02 — research cycle and adaptive choice

MV-T1 asks whether recent positive returns persist; MV-R1 asks whether recent negative returns reverse. Both disclose all parameter choices and windows, and use fixed signals for the fixture. They compete on usefulness of subsequent questions, not on authority. Freeze search family, universe, selection rule, data access history, cost model and expectation before calculation. ARH design and input references precede funded attempts. Execute through the public client, retain result or failure, settle, close and propose a hypothesis or next Theme using the supported classes.

Separate evaluator recomputes against exact inputs under its own funded grant and distinct lineage. ARH decides the validity of the check and research registration; only its applied result is accepted registration. A checked hypothesis registration remains a question for future testing. Failure and rejected proposals remain available through public history.

Next-choice rule for this slice: retain both method families; exclude previously rejected identical questions on the same dataset; prefer a question addressing a recorded failure mechanism over a parameter search on the same sample. Record candidates, selected question, rejected alternatives, reason, parents and exact evidence references through research records/selection. This can be deterministic. Changing the recipe creates MV-T2/MV-R2 and retains old contribution history. A selection Theme may require an owner decision: do not pretend P-1 covers all Theme registrations. The next scheduled cycle may wait there.

Method evaluation is a derived report of total metered cost, attempts, parameter variants, invalid-data incidents, reproducibility and useful follow-up questions, with unresolved evidence marked. Hypothesis assessment reports expectation versus observed outcome for the fixed research question. Strategy evaluation reports costs, turnover, drawdown, exposure and exit behavior. No composite score silently converts one to another.

Before dispatch, the work plan forecasts research, independent checks and any successor-Theme checks separately; ARH still owns every reservation and actual charge. A record of a negative result or a correctness PASS is not a permission to proceed past a failed strategy criterion. Owner research decisions and paper-strategy decisions are distinct items, even when displayed together. Inspecting immutable records and deterministic portfolio calculations add no synthetic research charge in the textual fixture; any metered calculation must instead have a named attempt. This fixture tariff is a proposed test input, not an assertion about provider pricing.

## C-03 — data and evaluation timing

Every run pins a data manifest: bytes/digest, source and revision, symbols and point-in-time membership, event times, availability bounds, retrieval time, calendar, adjustments and missingness. Historical Reconstruction uses virtual cutoffs and admits a value only if its documented availability upper bound is at/before the decision cutoff. Unknown availability makes the affected replay invalid, not merely low confidence. A later revision is usable only at its later availability. Store reuse/access counts and all searched variants; a held-out partition exposed by adaptive research becomes explored forever for that research family. Do not count repeated observations as independent samples.

Synthetic fixture data tests mechanics only. Historical holdout evaluation may justify a bounded paper experiment under a future accepted mandate; it never earns forward scientific standing. Future fresh evaluation needs a real commitment timestamp before the independently established earliest availability of every scoring value, pinned prediction/window/rule, source revision policy, delayed resolution/grace, missing-outcome handling and separate immutable outcome/evaluation records. ARH has no implemented public forward path for this; F-03 blocks that later scope. Never use synthetic clock advance as proof of real-time commitment.

Proposed ES-M1 admission to **paper-exploratory** requires executable immutable strategy specification; independent arithmetic check; valid input timing and disclosed reuse; chronological development/validation partition identified before the run; complete cost, slippage and adverse-cost sensitivity; declared limits and exit rule; and no unresolved reproduction or data defect. For the toy validation partition only, require net result >0 under both base and doubled cost, and observed drawdown ≤2%. These are operational fixture gates, not statistical proof of alpha. There is no minimum evidence escape for a passing calculation: missing partition provenance refuses admission. No strategy is labelled qualified on these fixtures.

A temporarily missing manifest may be recovered only as the exact content already committed before the experiment; a newly authored split cannot retroactively repair validation. Evidence access history is shared across the desk: a validation cohort revealed to either researcher is thereafter explored for both. Successor proposals must identify a genuinely unexposed cohort or explicitly remain exploratory. Simultaneously precommitted evaluations may finish before their results are shared, but later choices cannot call those same rows untouched.

## C-04 — strategy lifecycle

`candidate → evaluated → eligible → active → suspended → retiring → retired`, with rejected candidates retained. Evaluator commits a dossier, owner (or a separately admitted DA-S policy under C-12) admits eligible paper-exploratory status under ES-M1, portfolio boundary activates on first lawful nonzero allocation. The evaluator cannot admit. Rejection records failed criteria. Revised strategy is a new version with parent and zero inherited forward standing; predecessor history stays visible.

Suspension blocks new exposure immediately and sets target zero under M-1's standing exit instruction; pending buys must be cancelled/reconciled before reduction sizing. Owner may resume with a fresh dossier and current checks, returning to eligible; no automatic revival. Retirement follows an owner decision or separately admitted C-12 predeclared-window policy, first enters retiring, cancels opening orders, reduces confirmed positions and waits for all uncertain orders/fills to resolve. Retired requires flat attributed positions, no executable order and no unresolved fill liability. No retirement relabels old research. Strategy refusal records are Murmur records, not forged ARH refusals.

## C-05 — shared portfolio and allocation

At each approved slot the deterministic allocator gathers immutable strategy target requests and checks current mandate, eligible status, prices and portfolio revision. It commits one allocation batch or a recorded refusal. Two stages: clamp each request to 20% NAV, then proportionally scale requests sharing an underlying to 25%; finally scale all buys as needed for gross 40%, cash floor and turnover 20%. Use deterministic decimal quantities, round down to permitted lot size, leave residual cash; no randomized tie-breaking. Include fees and worst-case outstanding buys; do not assume pending sells free cash. Underlying identity is canonical across strategies and aliases; factor/group limits would need explicit mandate definitions before expansion.

Allocation records requested and admitted quantities, every binding constraint and before/after exposures. Position attribution is per strategy and underlying, while risk sums across strategies. No internal crosses in v1. Paper order identity `(allocation, strategy-version, instrument, side, ordinal)` is durable before submission, bound to one decision event and price model. Repeated submission recovers that order; new ID is not a retry. Current revision checks occur at order admission too. A stale allocation requires a new evaluated batch; old one remains history.

Fee-aware admission: first exclude ineligible strategy requests, retaining their refusal reasons. Set a conservative denominator to current marked NAV less the declared maximum fees/adverse slippage for the full eligible request set (including outstanding commitments, without double counting). Use that denominator for percentage exposure/cash tests and the fixed opening-day NAV for turnover. Apply the stated proportional clamps, then round each strategy down once to the permitted lot; do not redistribute leftover lots. Show them as unallocated cash. Refuse if cost/price bounds are absent. Revalidate the final rounded batch at its own worst permitted fills and costs; no cost deduction may be omitted merely because prices are unchanged. Any denominator ≤0 refuses.

An accepted batch records absolute targets, bounded costs, each child order identity and quantity, and execution progress. Before each new child admission, use the current portfolio revision and count all fills plus outstanding commitments of that batch once. Its own expected fills may advance progress without requiring an entirely new allocation, provided remaining children still attain the same targets within the original bounds and current limits. External incompatible changes invalidate the undispatched remainder; no silent rebasing of an already-submitted command is allowed. Lost-response recovery queries the original order; fill reconciliation remains permitted even if a newer mandate forbids further orders. An acknowledged fill cannot be discarded as stale. These rules do not grant cross-ledger atomicity or authorize a duplicate order.

Mark-to-market changes can breach limits without a new order. Suspend increases and record the breach. A required exit must cite an owner retirement/reduction decision or a mandate-authorized suspension/limit trigger and its exact position snapshot. The following **proposed exit exception** is separate from ordinary rebalancing; it remains subject to owner acceptance and is not part of the consumer-validation authorization.

For this long-only slice, the reduction predicate covers **gross dollar exposure, each underlying's long quantity and dollar exposure, and each strategy's dollar exposure**, including executable order remainders. Compare before/after at the same current marks; every measure must be non-increasing and at least one must decrease for a nonzero exit. Cancel/reconcile opening orders first. No short position, new instrument exposure, oversell or replacement opening order is permitted. Re-evaluate each continuation at current marks; old marks cannot authorize a new order. Factor/hedged portfolios need a new defined predicate before expansion.

Turnover, cumulative fees, realized loss and NAV drawdown are **not** monotone exposure measures: a lawful exit may increase them. Proposed M-1 authorizes required-exit turnover in addition to the ordinary daily 20% allowance, bounded by the remaining quantity of the exact exit obligation, never a fresh percentage allowance per retry. Ordinary turnover is absolute filled notional divided by opening-day NAV; outstanding orders reserve their maximum notional. Record ordinary and required-exit turnover separately and display their total. An exit cannot free the ordinary turnover already consumed or fund new exposure.

Each exit obligation pins current pre-exit NAV and a cash cost envelope: proposed M-1 maximum aggregate fees plus adverse slippage is **0.25% of that NAV**, fixed once for the entire obligation. Persist estimated, reserved, consumed and remaining cost; partial fills/restarts do not reset the envelope. At every order admission require a declared fee ceiling, minimum sale price and unencumbered cash sufficient to prefund the remaining envelope without assuming a future fill. No borrowing or use of research units is permitted. Exit costs may reduce NAV and thus worsen percentage-based concentration/drawdown readings even while the dollar-exposure predicate holds; record those residual breaches and keep increases suspended. The exit exception does not waive receipt reconciliation or current authority.

Cash precision for the proposed dollar fixture: use exact decimals; round a maximum permitted cash envelope down to cents, estimated/reserved cash liabilities up to cents, and quantities down to lots. Keep unrounded calculations in the evidence. Receipts must use the declared currency precision; invalid precision is a reconciliation discrepancy, not permission to change historical fees. Rounding never enlarges a grant or exit envelope.

If marks, cancellation status, cost bounds or sufficient cash are unavailable, retain the exit obligation and positions, refuse that order and request owner resolution. An owner may explicitly amend a cost envelope prospectively; the executor cannot do so. A risk stop never fabricates an exit price or flat book. S-18 exercises exit turnover above the ordinary limit and preserves the cost envelope across restart.

## C-06 — replaceable adapters

Backtest adapter takes exact strategy version, dataset manifest, information cutoff, calendar, fill/fee model and deterministic seed if relevant. Returns per-event target, orders/fills, gross/net results, turnover, drawdown, missing-data flags and trace to inputs. Must distinguish modelled results from observations; prohibit future-data access, hidden parameter searches and implicit data downloads. Independent evaluator reproduces from that closure. Select an existing engine only after a documented capability comparison against these requirements; no vendor is selected here.

Paper execution adapter accepts only admitted immutable orders, supports query by stable order ID, deduplicated submission, cancellation, partial fills, final status and replay of execution receipts. Receipts include unique fill ID, order, timestamp, quantity, price, fee and model version. Paper-book owner validates bounds and commits each receipt once, derives positions and cash, and preserves discrepancies. Refuse adapter startup if durable lookup or idempotency is absent. A contradictory receipt quarantines the order and blocks replacement; no assumed exactly-once network delivery. Synthetic textual fills below are examples, not an engine implementation.

## C-07 — concurrency, uncertainty and recovery

Before any side effect persist operation identity, command key, exact payload hash, actor, input references and expected revision. ARH client journal is required durable runtime material, never a cache to discard; use public outcome inspection to resolve lost replies. COMMITTED/REFUSED outcomes are reused. PARTIAL apply means reconcile its binding/obligations through public recovery; UNKNOWN may resend the same command key, never a fresh identity. A claim with no receipt is different: settle unknown at maximum and prohibit another execution of that attempt. Replacement needs ARH's quiescence/provider-disposition prerequisites and a new admitted attempt. “No duplicate charges” means replay does not charge twice; an explicit replacement legitimately adds cost.

ARH is authoritative for reservation races and its current base. Murmur does not subtract a dashboard budget to grant work. Concurrent separate operations can compete; same-operation multiwriter journal use is prohibited pending F-02 resolution. Funding-reference collisions require a successor proposal with fresh references, not pointer patching or funding by matching purpose.

Murmur uses its own serialized command boundary for portfolio and cycle revisions, durable request deduplication and exit obligations. These are domain responsibilities, not replacement ARH budget governance. Cross-boundary workflows are staged: persist intent → submit public command → resolve outcome → retain exact reference → commit next local transition. There is no global atomic commit. Incomplete ARH admission cannot cause local strategy admission. Revocation of Murmur standing authority is checked locally at each consequential transition; owner fences/revokes ARH grants separately. Pending obligations remain until each owner confirms disposition. Recovery never grants continuation merely because a queue contains old work.

## C-11 — identity boundary and orchestration candidate

Python and ARH's public Python client are the proposed foundation. LangGraph is the candidate coordinator, subject to S-15 and S-16; it creates no authority. Every resumed node establishes eligibility through the relevant public authoritative interface before its next side effect. Graph state never carries execution permission forward. Portfolio/risk/scheduling calculations are ordinary Python. UI and simulation engine remain unselected.

The bounded first slice guarantees only designated-identity role enforcement in a cooperative local process environment. R-T and R-R are distinct Agent Design identities/lineages, with no Checker grants, no decide/apply rights conferred by Murmur and no Principal delegation. CK is a third identity with its own grant; owner decisions are separate. Tests must refuse both self-check and cross-researcher check/approval. ARH may allow particular apply continuations under its own rules; those cannot grant a researcher the right to decide or independently admit.

Separate processes and tokens are required for the witness but are not proof of isolation: the local connection file holds all tokens and the same OS user can read it. Assumption: cooperative code uses only its designated connection; malicious credential theft and filesystem tampering are outside this slice's guarantee. Before untrusted agents, require a credential/OS boundary that prevents reading other roles' secrets and writing authoritative state, with adversarial runtime evidence. Do not claim isolation based on names, tokens or process separation alone.

Paid providers remain out of scope. An ordinary Python spending gateway could enforce hard limits only if it exclusively controls credentials, calls, cancellation and liability accounting; direct researcher provider access would defeat that boundary. Current ARH synthetic settlements and caller receipts are not proof of a hard cap on an external bill. Host-run admitted tools improve control of execution but do not alone establish vendor billing guarantees. Any future paid-provider scope requires a separate admitted contract and evidence.

## C-12 — proposed standing authority for an unattended desk

**Proposal DA-1, not admitted. Final scope interpretation: [B-01/NC-01–04](baseline-review.md) governs this proposal for ordinary follow-ups. A follow-up inside the exact existing Theme/grant uses research records and designs without a new Theme transition. DA-T applies only to genuine new/successor Themes; the two routine refinements do not justify requesting that extension. Exact initial Theme/grant fit must be established, never inferred from a label.** This is the current proposed alternative to W-2's per-instance owner decisions; W-2 remains the retained baseline. It changes proposed Murmur decision rights, not ARH CV1. No researcher, Checker, graph, credential or profitability score acquires general admission rights.

The owner approves one finite mandate and policy version before departure: two named research slots, exact grants/caps/expiry, permitted universe/data and method grammar, evaluation standard, admission/exit policies and independent identities. Inside this envelope routine questions, design revisions and subsequent investigations may proceed without a new human decision. Limits: two approaches, at most one successor question per approach per cycle, at most one replacement per interrupted operation, no new tools/providers, unchanged agent identities and no new grant. All variants/rejections/access history and cost remain visible. Existing per-grant/lineage caps still bind; limits do not create free computation. Changes outside this grammar are exceptions.

For this replay, permitted question grammar is trend/reversal over the same approved instruments, with either the predeclared volatility cohorts or the quiet-regime filter; only already-declared DEV/V1/V2 partitions may be used, respecting access history. Method recipe successors may vary those declared dimensions and must cite parents. Provider/model, executable tool bytes, evaluation criterion, universe, hidden parameter search, or timing-rule change is outside standing authority. A Murmur Method recipe is not an ARH Agent Design: changing the latter never transfers an old grant to the successor. P-1 Agent Design registration does not grant authorization.

Decision separation is mandatory: researcher proposes; CK independently checks with its own funded grant; a deterministic admission boundary applies only a currently valid policy. The boundary never does the research or invents/checks missing evidence. Every decision retains subject, policy/version, mandate, current revisions, exact independent check, evidence closure, funding refs and all failed criteria. A FAIL or STOP is never converted to PASS. New base means new check when upstream requires it; its cost must be funded or work pauses. An ambiguous economic judgment cannot be reduced to a generic confidence threshold.

## C-13 — research duration, holdings and authority expiry

Research slots and position holding periods are separate, adopting the owner's stated product preference. A batch closing never silently flattens, renews, reallocates or abandons holdings. Each strategy/allocation must declare its own holding deadline, monitoring cadence, mark staleness threshold, exit triggers, opening authority expiry and durable exit/receipt authority.

For DA-1's replay: research slots D1/D2 at18:00; each research grant expires at23:00 on its named day, independent of completion, no refill. Local completion stops new work immediately; the upstream grant may remain active until23:00 and must be displayed as such. Cycle2 may proceed after cycle1's reservations/obligations are resolved and local dispatch is stopped, without a routine fence. No predicate that requires a fence for actual supersession/revocation is waived.

T1's immutable paper window ends D1 at20:00, triggering DA-X and the exact W-2 exit. T2/R2 holding windows end D3 at20:00; new opening/rebalance authority expires D2 at21:00. At five-minute virtual monitoring slots while positions exist, fresh marks and known orders are checked; marks older than30 minutes block increases and trigger a data exception. No marks are fabricated to achieve an exit. Triggered retirement/limit reduction uses C-05. No routine discretionary adjustment is permitted after opening expiry.

Exit-only authority is separately preapproved for those exact versions and positions until flat/no outstanding executable order/no unresolved fill. It survives research/opening expiry and is revoked only by an explicit owner replacement of control with a recorded disposition for residual holdings. It cannot acquire exposure, renew an opening grant or reset the cost envelope. At D3 deadline cancel/reconcile outstanding opening instructions and create/resume the one exact exit obligation. If marks/status/cost limits prevent execution, retain the risk and escalate; the deadline cannot authorize a fabricated fill.

Omitting these duration/exit fields refuses allocation. Monitoring/exit rules are deterministic ordinary code when implemented, not hidden LLM research charges. Fresh processes read deadlines, cadence, exact quantities, order status, cost envelope and current authorities from owner records; missed monitoring slots are recorded and re-evaluated at current time without pretending observations occurred. A decision to keep positions beyond D3 needs the owner; a cost-driven loss alone is not a reason to rewrite the original hypothesis.

DA-1 scope closure: both cycles remain funded under the original explicitly authorized root Theme/SearchFamily scopes. New successor Themes document refined questions; neither their admission nor DA-M changes a grant scope. Hypotheses/results retain legitimate original-root motivation/scope as well as new ancestry references. A refinement requiring funding solely under a new, unauthorized Theme becomes an owner grant exception. DA-T must not add implicit descendant-scope inheritance.
